## 8️⃣ Practical industry recommendation

### For modern Kubernetes systems:

* **Logs** → stdout/stderr → Fluent Bit → Loki / Elastic
* **Metrics** → OpenTelemetry → Prometheus / SaaS
* **Traces** → OpenTelemetry → Tempo / Jaeger / SaaS

## 8️⃣ One sentence to remember forever

> **stdout/stderr is the pipe.
> OpenTelemetry is the language spoken inside the pipe.**
